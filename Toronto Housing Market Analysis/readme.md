# Toronto Housing Market Analysis

This project explores historical housing data in Toronto from 2005 to 2021 to uncover pricing trends, neighborhood patterns, and the effects of key events—such as the COVID-19 pandemic—on real estate dynamics. The analysis provides actionable insights through data visualization and statistical exploration.

---

## 📌 Objectives

- Analyze long-term pricing trends in Toronto’s housing market.
- Compare average home prices across different neighborhoods.
- Identify the impact of significant events (e.g., the 2008 financial crisis, COVID-19) on the housing market.
- Visualize market dynamics to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Python**: Data manipulation and analysis
- **pandas**: Data cleaning and transformation
- **seaborn & plotly**: Data visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook**: Interactive analysis and presentation
- **Kaggle Dataset**: Historical Toronto housing data (2005–2021)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Home prices in Toronto have shown a steady upward trend, with sharper increases after 2015.
- Certain neighborhoods consistently outperform others in terms of price growth and volatility.
- Economic events such as the 2008 crisis and COVID-19 had measurable but varying impacts on different areas.
- Data visualization helped identify potential areas of interest for investment or policy intervention.
